🏛️ UNIOSUN Identity & Key Dates

  • Licensing Date: December 21, 2006 (30th State University, 80th University in Nigeria).
  • Law Enacted: Passed in 2006 under Prince Olagunsoye Oyinlola.
  • Academic Inception: Students commenced in September 2007.
  • Foundation Day: September 21 annually.
  • 20th Anniversary Milestone: December 21, 2026.
  • Official Motto: "Living Spring of Knowledge and Culture" (Do NOT confuse with OAU's "For Learning and Culture").
  • Statutory Visitor: Executive Governor of Osun State, Sen. Ademola Jackson Nurudeen Adeleke.

  • Governance: 3 Senatorial Districts, 9 Federal Constituencies, 26 State Assembly seats.
  • First Civilian Governor: Sen. Isiaka Adetunji Adeleke (1992-1993).
  • First 4th Republic Governor: Chief Adebisi Akande (1999-2003).
  • Speaker (8th Assembly): Rt. Hon. Adewale Olumide Egbedun.
  • Chief Judge: Hon. Justice Oyebola Adepele Ojo.
  • SSG / CoS / HoS: SSG: Hon. Teslim Igbalaye; CoS: Alhaji Kazeem Akinleye; HoS: Elder Samuel Ayanleye Aina.
  • Landmarks: Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove (UNESCO World Heritage, 2005); Olumirin Waterfalls (Erin-Ijesha, Oriade LGA).

📚 Pedagogy, NUC & Assessment

  • Lecturer Triad: Teaching, Research, and Community Service.
  • CCMAS: Core Curriculum & Minimum Academic Standards (replaced BMAS). Split is 70% NUC National Core / 30% University Local Peculiarity.
  • Attendance Rule: Minimum 75% attendance required to sit for semester examinations.
  • 1 Credit Unit: 1 lecture hour per week for 15 weeks (15 contact hours); or 3 laboratory hours per week.
  • Academic Hierarchy: GA ➔ AL ➔ LII ➔ LI ➔ Senior Lecturer ➔ Reader (Assoc. Prof.) ➔ Professor.
  • Bloom's Taxonomy: Remembering ➔ Understanding ➔ Applying ➔ Analyzing ➔ Evaluating ➔ Creating.
  • Domains: Cognitive (Knowledge/Head), Affective (Values/Heart/Character), Psychomotor (Skills/Hand).
  • Grading Scale: A (70-100%, 5.0), B (60-69%, 4.0), C (50-59%, 3.0), D (45-49%, 2.0), E (40-44%, 1.0), F (0-39%, 0.0). 2:2 Degree = 2.40 to 3.49 CGPA.
  • Ethics: Salami Slicing = splitting one study into minimal papers; Fabrication = inventing fake data; Falsification = altering real data; Gift Authorship = co-author without intellectual contribution.

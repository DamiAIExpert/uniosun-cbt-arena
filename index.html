UNIOSUN High-Yield Cram Sheet

Fastest points to review right before entering the hall

Identity, Dates & Milestone

  • License Date: December 21, 2006 (30th State, 80th in Nigeria).
  • Law Enacted: Passed in 2006 under Prince Olagunsoye Oyinlola.
  • Students Commenced: September 2007.
  • Foundation Day: September 21 annually.
  • 20th Anniversary Milestone: December 21, 2026.
  • Official Motto: "Living Spring of Knowledge and Culture" (Do NOT confuse with OAU's "For Learning and Culture").
  • Statutory Visitor: Executive Governor Sen. Ademola Jackson Nurudeen Adeleke.

Current Principal Officers

  • Chancellor (3rd): Dr. (Mrs.) Victoria Olufunmilayo Samson (Mama BOVAS; succeeded Apostle Folorunso Alakija). Pioneer was late Oba Okunade Sijuwade.
  • Pro-Chancellor & Council Chairman: Prof. Wale Oladipo (Pioneer was Prof. Peter Okebukola).
  • Vice-Chancellor (4th Substantive): Prof. Odunayo Clement Adebooye (Specialist: Agronomy / Plant Physiology).
  • DVC (Academic - ARIP): Prof. Adetunji Lawrence Kehinde.
  • DVC (Administration & Development): Prof. Morufu O. Olayiwola.
  • Registrar (3rd Substantive): Mrs. Atinuke Abosede Oguntunde.
  • Bursar (3rd Substantive): Mr. Muideen Akintayo Lasisi.
  • Librarian (3rd Substantive): Dr. Olugbenga Wale Adewuyi.

Multi-Campus Distribution

  • Osogbo (Main, Oke-Baale): Science, Engineering & Technology (SET), Health Sciences, and Computing & IT (FCIT).
  • Okuku Campus: College of Management and Social Sciences.
  • Ikire Campus: College of Humanities and Culture.
  • Ejigbo Campus: College of Agriculture.
  • Ifetedo Campus: College of Law.
  • Ipetu-Ijesa Campus: College of Education.

Osun State Profile

  • Creation: August 27, 1991 by Gen. Ibrahim Babangida from old Oyo State.
  • LGAs: 30 LGAs + 1 Area Office (Ife East Area Office, Modakeke).
  • Districts/Seats: 3 Senatorial Districts, 9 Federal Constituencies, 26 House of Assembly seats.
  • First Civilian Governor: Sen. Isiaka Adetunji Adeleke (1992-1993).
  • First 4th Republic Governor: Chief Adebisi Akande (1999-2003).
  • Speaker (8th Assembly): Rt. Hon. Adewale Olumide Egbedun.
  • Chief Judge: Hon. Justice Oyebola Adepele Ojo.
  • Landmarks: Osun-Osogbo Grove (UNESCO 2005); Olumirin Waterfalls (Erin-Ijesha, Oriade LGA).

Pedagogy, NUC & Assessment

  • Lecturer Triad: Teaching, Research, and Community Service.
  • CCMAS: Replaced BMAS. Split: 70% NUC National Core / 30% Institutional Peculiarity.
  • Attendance Rule: Minimum 75% attendance required for exam eligibility.
  • Academic Hierarchy: GA ➔ AL ➔ LII ➔ LI ➔ Senior Lecturer ➔ Reader ➔ Professor.
  • Bloom's Taxonomy: Remembering ➔ Understanding ➔ Applying ➔ Analyzing ➔ Evaluating ➔ Creating.
  • Domains: Cognitive (Head), Affective (Heart/Character), Psychomotor (Hands/Skills).
  • Grading Scale: A (70-100%, 5.0), B (60-69%, 4.0), C (50-59%, 3.0), D (45-49%, 2.0), E (40-44%, 1.0), F (0-39%, 0.0). 2:2 is 2.40 to 3.49 CGPA.
  • Research Ethics: Salami slicing = fragmented publishing; Fabrication = inventing data; Falsification = altering data; Gift authorship = co-author without intellectual contribution.

National Leadership Current Affairs

  • Education Minister: Dr. Maruf Tunji Alausa (State: Prof. Suwaiba Said Ahmad).
  • NUC Executive Secretary: Prof. Abdullahi Yusufu Ribadu.
  • JAMB / TETFund / NELFUND: Prof. Ishaq Oloyede / Arc. Sonny Echono / Mr. Akintunde Sawyerr.
  • Digital Economy Minister: Dr. Bosun Tijani; NITDA DG: Kashifu Inuwa Abdullahi.
  • Chief Justice (CJN): Hon. Justice Kudirat Motonmori Olatokunbo Kekere-Ekun.
  • National Assembly: Senate President Godswill Akpabio; Speaker Tajudeen Abbas.
  • National Anthem: Lyrics by Lillian Jean Williams; Music by Frances Benda.
